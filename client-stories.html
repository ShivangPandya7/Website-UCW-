Business exitWealth Advisory and Asset Management

Turning a liquidity event into a durable plan.

The challenge

A second-generation promoter family in Gujarat sold a controlling stake in the manufacturing business they had built over three decades. The proceeds, a number that felt abstract until it landed in an account, needed to become something durable, not merely liquid.

How UpperCrust helped

UpperCrust staged the capital into UCWF over eight weeks, set aside a bond ladder for the next three years of known expenses, and built mutual fund plans sized to each family member’s own goals.

The outcome

Today the family has one advisor across all of it, and one review where every number makes sense.

Next generationWealth Advisory

Bringing the next generation into the room.

The challenge

A trading family had always made investment decisions informally: the father made the calls, and the rest of the family found out afterwards. As his son and daughter took on more of the business, that stopped working for everyone.

How UpperCrust helped

UpperCrust moved the quarterly review to the family table, with both generations present. Each member now sees their own goals inside the family portfolio, and the reporting explains why the portfolio holds what it holds, not just what it is worth.

The outcome

The approach stayed the same. What changed is who understands it.

NRI investingWealth Advisory and Asset Management

One relationship, instead of five scattered ones.

The challenge

An NRI professional in Dubai had India holdings spread across four distributors and eleven mutual fund schemes. Nobody had the full picture, including him.

How UpperCrust helped

UpperCrust consolidated everything into a single view, rationalised the eleven schemes into a coherent five-fund plan, and added a UCWF mandate as the core of his India equity.

The outcome

Reviews now happen over video, on his schedule, with everything visible in one place.

Staying the courseAsset Management

Staying invested when it was hardest to.

The challenge

During a sharp correction, a long-standing UCGF client called wanting to exit entirely.

How UpperCrust helped

Rather than simply processing the redemption, the UpperCrust team walked her through what the fund is built to do in exactly this kind of market, and what selling at the bottom would lock in.

The outcome

She stayed invested, and the portfolio recovered over the following quarters. The call did not change the fund’s process; it is what the process is for.
